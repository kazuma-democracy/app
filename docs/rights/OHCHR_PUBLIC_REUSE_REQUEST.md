# Request for permission to reuse narrow OHCHR settlement-related business data

To: United Nations Publications Rights and Permissions (`permissions@un.org`), with routing to the appropriate OHCHR rights holder if required.

Subject: Permission request for limited normalized OHCHR data in the free/open-source WA Commons browser extension

WA Commons is an open-source project that presents source-grounded company evidence and user-selected policy results without a universal moral score. We request written permission to redistribute a narrow normalized subset of the OHCHR business-enterprise material concerning specified settlement-related activities in the occupied Palestinian territory.

Requested fields are limited to: company name or identifier where present; the OHCHR-defined activity category; source/publication date; source document or page locator; and attribution/provenance needed to identify OHCHR as the source. We do not request permission to redistribute raw PDFs, full source documents, images, or unrelated text.

The extension will preserve the source-defined scope and status. It will not convert the OHCHR material into a generic `supports Israel`, `anti-Israel`, or similar political label, and it will keep unresolved identity, disputed evidence, and unavailable coverage explicit.

The intended distribution is inside signed browser-extension packages for Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. The project is currently affiliate-free and the browser client does not require an account or telemetry. Source links and a correction/challenge route remain visible.

Please confirm whether the requested normalized fields may be reproduced and redistributed in this form, what attribution wording is required, whether any field-specific or third-party restrictions apply, and whether future updates require a renewed permission request.
